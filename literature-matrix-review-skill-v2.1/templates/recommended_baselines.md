# Recommended Baselines

## Baseline selection principle

Choose baselines from the literature matrix, not arbitrarily.

## Categories

1. Exact solver / MIP baseline for small instances.
2. Simple dispatching rules for basic comparison.
3. Literature-specific heuristic for the closest problem stream.
4. General metaheuristic baseline.
5. Ablation variants of the proposed method.
