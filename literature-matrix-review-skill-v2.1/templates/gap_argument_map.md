# Gap Argument Map

## Production fact

Describe the real production mechanism or business object transformation.

## Why it matters

Explain why this mechanism changes scheduling decisions, object definitions, release times, or feasibility.

## Scheduling consequence

Explain the resulting modeling issue: e.g., non-independent jobs, many-to-one releases, AND-precedence, skipped stages, batch formation.

## Existing literature handles

Group existing literature by what it already handles.

## Remaining modeling gap

Describe what remains insufficient after considering all groups together.

## Why this gap is not a trivial combination

Explain why simply combining keywords or constraints is insufficient.

## How this study responds

Map the study's response to problem definition, model, algorithm, and experiments.

## Evidence papers

List the supporting papers and the exact evidence they provide.

## Possible reviewer challenge

What might a reviewer question?

## Response to challenge

How can the paper answer the challenge?
