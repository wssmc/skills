# Related Work Synthesis Template

```text
[Topic sentence defining the shared research stream.]

[Group 1 synthesis: studies addressing a common problem feature or method family.]

[Group 2 synthesis/contrast: studies extending or differing on a specific dimension.]

[Closest-study comparison: why Study X is closest and exactly how it differs.]

[Local synthesis: what this stream has established.]

[Local gap/transition: what remains unresolved and why it matters for the present study.]
```

Avoid author-by-author stacking unless each cited study is a genuine turning point.
