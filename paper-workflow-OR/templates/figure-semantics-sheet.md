## Visual quality check

- no text, formula, symbol, block, connector, arrowhead, legend, or panel boundary obscures another;
- labels and relation endpoints remain clear at final manuscript size;
- each panel is individually cited in the main text when it conveys a distinct point;
- source assets, assembled layout, and rendered manuscript page have been inspected when applicable.

For a flowchart, also confirm explicit start/end nodes, correct process/decision shapes, consistent `Y`/`N` branches, and no action text on branch labels. For a neighborhood illustration, confirm before/after states, selected elements, transformation direction, and unobstructed positions.
