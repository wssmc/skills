# Cover Letter Template

```text
Dear Editor,

Please consider our manuscript entitled “[TITLE]” for publication in [JOURNAL].

[Short paragraph: studied problem and why it matters.]

[Short paragraph: 2–3 principal contributions.]

[Short paragraph: main evidence and why the work fits the journal.]

[Optional verified submission declarations.]

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
[Corresponding author]
[Affiliation]
[Email]
```

Do not insert originality/exclusive-submission/authorship/conflict declarations unless confirmed.
