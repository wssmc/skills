# Journal Profiles

> **Status:** Placeholder for future journal-specific submission profiles.

Future files may include:

```text
COR.md
EJOR.md
JOS.md
SWEVO.md
ESWA.md
...
```

A journal profile may define:
- article type and structure overrides;
- abstract/keyword limits;
- Highlights requirements;
- graphical abstract requirements;
- figure/table/caption constraints;
- double-blind requirements;
- data/code availability statements;
- CRediT / funding / conflict declarations;
- reference/citation style;
- submission-file organization;
- AI-use disclosure requirements;
- supplementary-material rules.

Until a profile exists, consult the target journal's current author guidelines and do not infer submission rules from another journal.
