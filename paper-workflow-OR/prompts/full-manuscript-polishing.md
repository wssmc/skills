# Full-Manuscript Polishing Prompt

Polishing is the final harmonization stage. It does not replace the Writing Specifications and must not independently change scientific claim strength.

## Pass 1 — Manuscript harmonization

Check:
- terminology consistency;
- abbreviation consistency;
- problem/method/component naming;
- tense consistency;
- cross-section repetition;
- paragraph transitions;
- figure/table/algorithm naming;
- project terminology glossary.

## Pass 2 — Sentence-level polishing

Improve:
- sentence clarity;
- syntax variety;
- excessive nominalization;
- mechanical passive voice;
- formulaic transitions;
- redundant wording;
- local awkwardness.

Preserve:
- equations;
- symbols;
- numbers;
- citations;
- objective;
- problem boundary;
- result magnitude;
- statistical significance status.

If a sentence appears scientifically overclaimed or unnecessarily defensive, flag it for Audit rather than silently changing the scientific claim.
