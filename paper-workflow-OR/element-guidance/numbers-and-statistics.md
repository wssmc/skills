# Numbers and Statistics Guidance

## State aggregation explicitly

Prefer:

> `Across the 90 Large instances, the mean instance-level RPD is 0.52%.`

rather than:

> `The average RPD is 0.52%.`

## Distinguish best, mean, median, and best-of-runs

These are not interchangeable.

## Percentage vs percentage points

From 5% to 3%:
- difference = 2 percentage points;
- relative reduction = 40%.

## Runtime

Define:
- CPU vs wall time;
- per-run vs total;
- mean vs median;
- initialization included/excluded;
- solver-only vs full script.

## Statistical significance

Do not use `significantly` from a mean difference alone.

State:
- test;
- analysis unit;
- correction;
- p-value/effect/rank;
- practical magnitude when relevant.

## Average rank

A lower rank does not automatically mean statistically significant superiority.

## W/T/L

Always define:
- comparator;
- tie rule;
- analysis unit.

## Rounding

Use consistent precision. Manuscript prose may use fewer decimals than tables if ranking/interpretation is preserved.
