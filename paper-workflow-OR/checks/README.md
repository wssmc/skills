# Checks

Checks validate generated or revised manuscript content against the Structure Specification, Writing Specifications, Element Guidance, and Truthfulness rules.

Files:
- `section-check.md`
- `full-manuscript-check.md`
- `peer-review-criteria.md`
- `problem-abstraction-check.md`
- `contribution-check.md`
- `pseudocode-maturity-check.md`

## Section Check

Answers:

> Is this chapter/section complete, clear, and internally correct?

## Full-Manuscript Check

Answers:

> Does the full paper describe one coherent problem, method, and evidence chain?

Multiple Section Checks do not replace a Full-Manuscript Check because cross-section consistency must also be tested.

## Severity

- `BLOCKING`
- `MAJOR`
- `MINOR`
- `SUGGESTION`

## Claim calibration

Full-manuscript audit also classifies important wording as:
- `TOO STRONG`
- `APPROPRIATELY STATED`
- `UNNECESSARILY DEFENSIVE`
